```
AI video tools split into three categories, and they don't compete:

1. **AI avatars** — a realistic digital presenter reads your script.
   → Synthesia (enterprise standard), HeyGen (clone yourself)

needed an avatar, or vice versa.

out fast can cost more than a pricier plan that covers your output.

- One avatar tool (Synthesia or HeyGen) for talking-head content
```

**发布位置**：medium.com/@buigialy30206

**标题**：The Real Cost of AI Video Tools in 2026 (It's Not the Sticker Price)

**正文**：

```
Everyone quotes AI video tool prices — $8, $12, $22 a month — as if the
sticker price is what you pay. It isn't.

The number that actually matters is your **credit burn rate**.

## How the Credit System Actually Works

You pay a monthly fee and get a fixed number of credits. Every video you
generate consumes credits, and the burn varies wildly:

- A short talking-head clip might cost a handful of credits
- A longer generated sequence can burn a big chunk
- Higher resolution and longer durations burn faster

Run out before month-end, and you face two options: wait until the reset,
or upgrade to a higher tier.

## The Math People Get Wrong

A cheap $8 plan that runs out of credits in two weeks forces an upgrade —
or worse, stalls your content schedule. A $22 plan that comfortably covers
your monthly output is cheaper in practice.

Before you pay, estimate: how many videos do you actually produce per
month? Then pick the plan whose credit allowance covers it, not the one
with the lowest headline price.

## The Two-Tool Setup

For most creators, the efficient stack is two tools:
- One avatar tool (Synthesia for stock avatars, HeyGen to clone yourself)
- One generation tool (Runway or Pika) for creative footage

Pika — hidden fees included — here: https://aivideotest.com/pricing*
```
