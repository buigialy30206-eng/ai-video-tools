The Short Answer

AI video tools start at $8/month (Pika) and climb to $24+ (HeyGen, Synthesia). The real cost isn't the sticker price — it's whether your monthly credit allowance covers your actual output without forcing an upgrade.

💡 Quick Tip: Calculate cost-per-video, not monthly price. A $24 tool that makes 20 videos costs $1.20/video — cheap. A $12 tool that bottlenecks you at 5 videos is the real expense.

The Credit System Trap

The biggest hidden cost across all AI video tools is the credit system. You pay a monthly fee, get a fixed number of credits, and each video consumes them. Run out before month-end and you either wait or upgrade — this is why the specific tool you pick matters more than its sticker price.

The trap: a $12/month plan looks cheap until you realize it only covers 15 minutes of generated video, and you need 60 minutes for a serious channel. Suddenly you're on the $50 tier.

Always check: how many minutes of video does the base plan actually produce?

The Real Cost of Running a Channel

  • Casual creator (4 videos/month): Pika or D-ID, $8-15/month — under $20 total
  • Active creator (15 videos/month): HeyGen or Synthesia, $24-48/month — you'll need the mid tier
  • Full-time channel (30+ videos/month): two tools, $50-100/month — the credit ceiling forces an upgrade

How to Pick the Right Price Tier

  1. Estimate your monthly video count first — buy based on output, not features
  2. Start on the free trial — test the credit burn rate
  3. Calculate cost-per-video — a $24 tool making 20 videos costs $1.20/video
  4. Avoid annual plans until 3 months in — most people switch tools once — see how the top tools compare before committing

Bottom Line

The "cheapest" tool isn't the one with the lowest monthly price — it's the one whose credits actually cover your output without forcing an upgrade. For most creators, the sweet spot is a $20-30/month plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the cheapest AI video generator?

Pika is the cheapest at around $8 per month, followed by Runway at $12. But the real cost depends on your output volume — a cheap plan that runs out of credits quickly can end up costing more than a pricier plan that covers your needs.

Do AI video tools have free tiers?

Most offer free trials rather than permanent free tiers. Free trials let you test the workflow and credit burn rate before committing. Some tools like Pictory have free tiers with watermarks.

Why do AI video tools use credits?

Credits meter your usage because AI video generation is computationally expensive. Each generation consumes GPU time, so tools ration it through credits. Understanding your monthly credit allowance is key to avoiding surprise upgrades.

Is an annual plan worth it?

Usually yes if you've used the tool for 3+ months and know it fits your workflow. Annual plans typically save 20-30%. But avoid committing annually before you've validated that the tool actually covers your monthly output.
