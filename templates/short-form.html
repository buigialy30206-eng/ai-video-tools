The Short Answer

The best AI tools for TikTok and Shorts are Pika for budget clip generation, HeyGen for talking-head shorts, and Pictory for repurposing long content. Pair any of them with CapCut for captions.

💡 Quick Tip: The hook in the first 2 seconds matters more than the video quality. AI generates the visuals — the hook is on you.

Short-form video is where the growth is — but it's also where the volume demand is brutal. TikTok and YouTube Shorts reward creators who post daily, and the only way to keep up is with AI.

What Short-Form Creators Actually Need

Short-form is a different beast from long-form. You need speed, captions (85% is watched muted), a hook in the first 2 seconds, and vertical 9:16 format. AI tools solve all four.

The Short-Form Workflow

  1. Generate the clip — Pika (visual hook) or HeyGen (talking head)
  2. Add auto-captions — CapCut or the tool's built-in captions
  3. Add a strong hook — the first 2 seconds decide everything
  4. Export vertical — 9:16, always
  5. Post to TikTok + Shorts + Reels — one clip, three platforms

The Hook Is Everything

AI generates the video, but the hook is on you. A short-form video with a weak first 2 seconds gets swiped past no matter how good the AI output is. Pair a strong hook with AI visuals, and you have a daily posting system.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best free AI tool for TikTok videos?

CapCut is the best free option — its auto-captions, templates and AI effects are the industry standard for short-form editing. For generation, Pika offers a low-cost entry point for clip creation.

Can AI tools make viral shorts?

AI generates the visuals, but virality depends on your hook and topic. A strong first 2 seconds matters more than video quality. AI removes the production barrier so you can test more ideas faster.

How do I add captions to AI videos?

Most AI video tools include auto-captions. CapCut has the best free auto-captioning for short-form. Captions are essential because 85% of short-form video is watched on mute.

What's the best format for TikTok videos?

Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, 1080x1920 pixels. Short-form platforms are phone-first, so always export vertical. Keep videos under 60 seconds for maximum reach on TikTok and Shorts.

How many shorts should I post per week?

Consistency beats volume. Three to five quality shorts per week outperforms daily low-effort posting. AI removes the production barrier, so your real limit is how many strong hooks and ideas you can generate, not how fast you can render.
