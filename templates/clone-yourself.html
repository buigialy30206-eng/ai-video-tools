What You Need Before You Start

Cloning quality depends entirely on your source footage. Garbage in, garbage out. Before you record:

  • Good lighting — face fully lit, no harsh shadows
  • A plain background — solid color, nothing distracting
  • A stable camera — tripod or propped phone
  • Clear audio — quiet room, minimal echo

You only need 2 minutes of footage — but those 2 minutes should be your best take, because the clone inherits how you look in that clip forever.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should my cloning video be?

About 2 minutes. HeyGen needs roughly 2 minutes of clear footage of you talking naturally to build an accurate digital twin. Longer footage doesn't significantly improve the clone, but clearer footage does.

Is AI cloning legal?

Cloning yourself is legal. Cloning someone else without their consent raises legal and ethical issues. Always clone only yourself or people who have given explicit written permission.

How much does HeyGen avatar cloning cost?

HeyGen's avatar cloning requires a paid plan, starting around $24 per month. The clone itself also consumes credits each time you generate a video with it.

Can I use my AI clone on YouTube?

Yes, but disclose synthetic media. YouTube requires creators to flag realistic AI-generated content that could be mistaken for a real person. Use YouTube's altered content disclosure to stay compliant.
